# Embryo Waves

Embryo Waves is Embryo-Zero's fork of [GTKWave](https://github.com/gtkwave/gtkwave) (e40ba79, commit e40ba79). It shows simulation signals as waveforms.
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING, LICENSE, LICENSE.TXT) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
